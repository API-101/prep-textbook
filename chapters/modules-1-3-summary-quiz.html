Demand, Supply, and Equilibrium

Review the core market model before moving into curve shifters.

Summary

Modules 1-3 build the basic supply-and-demand model. Demand describes how buyers respond to price; supply describes how producers respond to price; equilibrium is the price and quantity where the two sides of the market align.

The CAP example shows why this benchmark matters. A price support above equilibrium raises the price farmers receive, reduces quantity demanded, increases quantity supplied, and creates a surplus that government must absorb.
