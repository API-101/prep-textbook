API-101: Markets and Market Failures

The goal of these materials is to prepare you for API-101 at the Harvard Kennedy School, a core course in the MPP program. This learning experience will help ensure that you and your classmates have a similar foundation in several core principles of microeconomics.

Work through the three modules in order. Each module builds on the previous one, guiding you from the basics of demand and supply through market equilibrium, shifters, and welfare analysis. Consider allocating 2–5 hours depending on your previous economics experience.

Modules

Module 1 · 3 Parts
The Basics of Demand, Supply, and Equilibrium
Learn the fundamental relationship between price and quantity. Graph demand and supply curves, solve for market equilibrium, and connect these tools to real policy through the EU's Common Agricultural Policy.
~60 min Interactive graphs Practice quiz
  • Part 1: Interpreting and graphing demand
  • Part 2: Interpreting and graphing supply
  • Part 3: Solving for equilibrium + CAP policy
Module 2
Demand and Supply Shifters
Explore what happens when factors beyond price change. Learn to shift demand and supply curves, find new equilibrium points, and apply the four-step process for analyzing market changes.
~80 min Interactive graphs Practice quiz
  • Factors that shift demand curves
  • Factors that shift supply curves
  • The four-step process for equilibrium changes
  • Policy connection: CAP and supply shifters
Module 3
Consumer and Producer Surplus
Understand how markets create value for both buyers and sellers. Calculate consumer and producer surplus graphically, evaluate market efficiency, and see how policy interventions redistribute welfare.
~70 min Interactive graphs Practice quiz
  • Consumer surplus and willingness to pay
  • Producer surplus and willingness to sell
  • Social surplus and allocative efficiency
  • Policy connection: CAP and surplus redistribution

By the End of These Modules, You Will Be Able To

  • Understand how the quantity of a good or service supplied or demanded is related to its price
  • Manipulate a demand or supply equation and plot it on a graph
  • Plot supply and demand together and find the equilibrium point
  • Explain shifts in supply or demand and how they impact a market's equilibrium
  • Calculate consumer and producer surplus using graphical methods

How to Get the Most Out of These Modules

Set Aside Focused Time
Work in a quiet place without distractions. Each module is designed to be completed in one sitting.
Work Through In Order
The modules build on each other. Complete Module 1 before moving to Module 2, and Module 2 before Module 3.
Engage with Interactives
Manipulate the graphs, try different values, and sketch your own curves. Active engagement deepens understanding.
Use Self-Checks
Take advantage of self-checks and practice quizzes as opportunities to test your understanding and learn from feedback.
