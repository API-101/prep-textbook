The goal of these materials is to prepare you for API-101 at the Harvard Kennedy School, a core course in the MPP program. This online learning experience will help ensure that you and your classmates have a similar foundation in several core principles of microeconomics.

We ask that you please complete the modules by Sunday, August 24, before the start of orientation week. Faculty will track your progress and completion. On your first week of classes, you will take an online quiz on this material, which will count towards 5% of your course grade. You should complete the modules in order. Consider allocating 2-5 hours for this preparation work, depending on your previous economics experience.

How to Get the Most Out of Each Module

Best practices

  • Set aside time that will let you focus, and work in a quiet place without distraction.
  • Use a modern browser on a strong internet connection via a computer (not a mobile device).
  • On each page, you will encounter videos, diagrams, text, and images. 
  • Please engage with each page from top to bottom.
  • Go at a pace that suits your familiarity with the material.

 

Navigation

  • The modules can be easily navigated by using the Next and Previous buttons at the bottom of the page. 
  • The Home link in the left-hand menu will always take you back to this page. 
  • Use the Modules link to navigate to a specific part of the modules. 
  • The videos are somewhat brief and condensed. You can pause and rewind, turn on captions, and view them at full screen and different speeds.

 

How to Check Your Learning

The modules offer several opportunities to check your learning, including self-checks and quizzes. Although the quizzes generate quiz scores, your credit is based only on completion of each question rather than the score.

  • Do your best on each question.
  • Take time to think through your answers.
  • Take advantage of feedback as an opportunity to learn more.
