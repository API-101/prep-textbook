Wrapping Up


This concludes this sequence of three modules. You should now be able to:

  • Understand how the quantity of a good or service supplied or demanded is related to its price
  • Manipulate a demand or supply equation and plot it on a graph
  • Plot supply and demand together and find the equilibrium point
  • Explain shifts in supply or demand and how they may impact a market's equilibrium point
  • Calculate consumer and producer surplus
