Welcome to the first of three modules designed to prepare you for API-101 at the Harvard Kennedy School. This online learning experience will help ensure that you and your classmates have a similar foundation in some of the core principles of microeconomics. The modules, which should be viewed in order, will guide you through the following concepts:

  • Module 1: The Basics of Supply, Demand, and Equilibrium (you are here)
  • Module 2: Demand and Supply Shifters
  • Module 3: Consumer and Producer Surplus

Each module has a similar structure. A reading about the assigned concepts will be followed by several video demonstrations providing further detail and explaining the relevant calculations and graphing. Self-check opportunities will be provided throughout the modules. Each module will conclude with a practice quiz. A policy example continually connects the math to the realm of public policy. 

Faculty will track your progress and completion of the modules. Consider allocating 2-5 hours for the entire sequence.

A Reminder: How to Get the Most Out of This Module

Best practices:

  • The module can be easily navigated by using the Next and Previous buttons at the bottom of the page. 
  • Use video settings to control speed, captioning, etc.
  • Use self-checks to test your knowledge before moving on.
  • The required practice quiz is an opportunity to get feedback and learn more.
  • The time estimated for this module is 60 minutes, though your experience may vary. If you are deeply experienced in this content, you may move through it quickly as a review.  If the content is not familiar, you may need more time to learn the concepts.
