Welcome to the second of three modules designed to prepare you for API-101 at the Harvard Kennedy School.

In the first module, you were introduced to the relationship between the price of a good or service and the quantity supplied or demanded. But what about factors other than price? In this module, you'll begin with some reading and then watch several videos demonstrating how these additional factors can influence (or "shift") the supply or demand. We will continue to use European Union's Common Agricultural Policy as an example.

 

A Reminder: How to Get the Most Out of This Module

Best practices:

  • The module can be easily navigated by using the Next and Previous buttons at the bottom of the page. 
  • Use video settings to control speed, captioning, etc.
  • Use self-checks to test your knowledge before moving on.
  • The required practice quiz is an opportunity to get feedback and learn more.
  • The time estimated for this module is 80 minutes, though your experience may vary. If you are deeply experienced in this content, you may move through it quickly as a review.  If the content is not familiar, you may need take more time to learn the concepts.
